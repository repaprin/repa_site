PRIN 2022 Research Project

REPA — Artificial Intelligence for Process Analytics

REPA explores the integration of Artificial Intelligence, Process Mining and data-driven analytics to support healthcare organizations in understanding, monitoring and improving complex operational and clinical processes.

Discover the project

Project overview

About the project

Healthcare organizations generate large volumes of heterogeneous operational data. REPA aims to transform these data into actionable knowledge by combining Artificial Intelligence techniques with Process Analytics and Process Mining methodologies.

The project investigates how intelligent data-driven approaches can improve process transparency, organizational efficiency and clinical decision-making in healthcare environments.

Research objectives

Main goals

Process Mining

Analyze healthcare workflows through event logs and process-aware analytical models.

Artificial Intelligence

Develop predictive and explainable AI techniques for healthcare process optimization.

Decision Support

Provide actionable insights to healthcare professionals and decision-makers.

Scientific methodology

Methodological approach

The REPA framework combines event log extraction, process analytics, machine learning and AI-based reasoning techniques to model, monitor and improve healthcare processes.

ECML PKDD 2026 Workshop

AI4PM 2026 — Artificial Intelligence for Process Mining

Naples, Italy · September 7, 2026

AI4PM 2026 is a full-day workshop co-located with ECML PKDD 2026, bringing together researchers and practitioners working at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Process Mining.

The workshop is organized with the participation of the University of Bari Aldo Moro, University of Milan and University of Trieste, together with Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München.

Panel

Multimodal Process Mining in Healthcare

A dedicated panel on “Multimodal Process Mining in Healthcare” is currently being organized within the workshop, focusing on emerging approaches that combine heterogeneous and multimodal healthcare data with Process Mining and Artificial Intelligence.

Panelists include:
