Project overview

Turning healthcare process data into actionable knowledge

Healthcare organizations operate through highly complex processes involving patients, clinical activities, administrative procedures, resources and decision points. These processes generate large volumes of digital traces, but the resulting data are often fragmented, noisy and difficult to interpret.

REPA aims to bridge this gap by combining Process Mining, Process Analytics and Artificial Intelligence. The project focuses on extracting event logs from healthcare data, discovering and analyzing process behavior, predicting future states, and supporting decision-making through explainable AI-based models.

The final objective is to provide healthcare stakeholders with methods and tools for monitoring processes, identifying bottlenecks, understanding deviations, supporting resource allocation and improving the quality and efficiency of healthcare services.

Research goals

Scientific and operational objectives

Process understanding

Discover, model and analyze healthcare workflows from event data, highlighting process variants, frequent patterns and deviations.

Predictive analytics

Develop AI/ML models able to anticipate process outcomes, delays, risks and resource needs across clinical and organizational pathways.

Explainable decision support

Design transparent and interpretable analytics so that results can be understood and trusted by domain experts and healthcare decision-makers.

Data quality

Address heterogeneity, incompleteness and uncertainty in healthcare data before transforming them into reliable event logs.

Optimization

Support process improvement by identifying bottlenecks, inefficiencies, deviations and opportunities for better resource allocation.

Knowledge transfer

Produce reusable methods, scientific outputs and dissemination activities for the research and healthcare communities.

Methodology

From raw healthcare data to process intelligence

The project follows a pipeline that connects data acquisition, event-log construction, process analytics, AI/ML modeling and operational decision support.

01

Data acquisition

Collect and integrate heterogeneous healthcare data sources describing activities, actors, timestamps and process context.

02

Event log construction

Transform raw data into structured event logs suitable for Process Mining and analytics tasks.

03

Process analytics

Discover process models, analyze variants, measure performance and detect deviations from expected behavior.

04

AI and ML models

Train predictive, prescriptive and explainable models to support monitoring, forecasting and recommendation.

05

Decision support

Translate analytical results into indicators, evidence and recommendations for healthcare managers and professionals.
