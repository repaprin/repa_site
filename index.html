The project

AI and Process Analytics to support healthcare organizations

Healthcare organizations generate large amounts of data related to clinical pathways, resources, activities and operational decisions. REPA aims to transform these data into actionable knowledge through analytical models and AI/ML techniques supporting process monitoring, prediction and improvement.

The project focuses on Process Mining scenarios in healthcare, with particular attention to data quality, process discovery, prediction, recommendation and explainability of results.
